PlaStim

Control for FOC-Stim boxes: ET-312 patterns on two independent channels, from this computer or the M5 remote.

Getting started

  1. Plug your FOC-Stim into this computer with USB and switch it on. Use a data cable: a charge-only cable shows nothing.
  2. Check its firmware. The box needs the PlaStim firmware (v7 or later) for two channels and the pulse shapes. If it shows stock, flash it on the Boxes tab (switch the M5 remote off and take the electrodes off first).
  3. Connect this computer's engine to the box.
    Looking for boxes…
  4. Open the player and play:
    1. Pick a pattern (Waves or Stroke are good first ones).
    2. Turn the box's own knob low: it is the master limit on everything this app sends.
    3. Put the electrodes on, then press ARM: the output ramps up slowly from zero.
    4. Raise Level A and Level B a little at a time. MA changes the pattern's speed and feel, not its strength.
    5. STOP at any time: the red button, Space in the player, or Pause anywhere on this computer.
    Open player
  5. Optional. Pop out in the player's top bar keeps a small always-on-top window with STOP and the levels. The M5 remote plays the same patterns without this computer.

Safety first

  • Never place electrodes on or across the chest, head or neck; keep the current path below the waist.
  • Do not use with a pacemaker or other implanted electronics, a heart condition, epilepsy, or when pregnant.
  • Start with everything at zero and increase slowly. Stop at any pain or discomfort.
  • Never while driving, operating machinery, asleep, or in water.
  • If the box stops by itself (a safety trip), switch it off and on again and start from zero.

        If a flash fails
        
    A failed flash leaves the box in its built-in bootloader, which lives in read-only memory: the box can always
          be flashed again. If it no longer answers at all, switch it off, hold the STM32 boot button while switching
          it on, then flash again (the flasher finds the bootloader already active). Switch the remote off first.
          Keep a known-good image to go back to.

      ET-312 built-in modes 
      
      
      The ET-312's own 18 modes (Waves, Stroke, Climb, ...) are programs inside ErosTek's firmware, so
        they are not included here. If you own an ET-312, you can extract them from its firmware; they stay on this
        computer.
      
      
        
        
      1. Get your ET-312B's firmware as a decrypted v1.6 image (a .bin, or an Intel .hex). The
          buttshock project's tools can download it from your own box
          (scripts/fw-utils.py --downloadfw).
        2. 
        
      2. Choose the file and press Extract. Only the mode programs are kept, in
          ; the image itself is not stored.
        3. 
        
      3. Press Load patterns & settings to put the modes on the M5 remote. The player shows them after the
          engine is started again (disconnect and connect the box).

      Box Wi-Fi
      
      
      Point a box at the remote's own Wi-Fi (one hop, no house access point in between), or back at the
        house Wi-Fi. Done over the box's USB; the box never starts its output for this. Switch the remote on first:
        the box gives up on a network after two tries.
